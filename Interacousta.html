IA · Internet of Sounds

Browse over sound with 4-FSK or Bell 202 AFSK. Remote stays offline — only the Router needs the network. 4-FSK runs at only a few bps, so pages are short excerpts; Bell 202 runs at 1200 baud and is far faster.

Settings

4-FSK — 1000 / 1400 / 1800 / 2200 Hz · 10 or 20 sym/s. Bell 202 — classic AFSK 1200 baud · mark 1200 Hz / space 2200 Hz. Use the same modulation on both devices.
When On, the Router follows each page with up to 10 page images as Robot 36 SSTV (~37 s per pass). Images are sent in strict order 1→10, then 1→10 again. The Remote merges both rounds and lists any image numbers still missing. Set On on both devices. Off keeps pages text-only.
Router receives requests, loads pages, and transmits content over the acoustic link. Remote stays offline and displays pages delivered by sound only.
